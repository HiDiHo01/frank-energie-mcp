# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We expect contributors, maintainers, and users to act respectfully and professionally.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- being constructive in reviews and discussions
- focusing on the issue, not the person
- respecting differing technical opinions
- giving clear, actionable feedback
- being patient with new contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, or abuse
- insulting or demeaning language
- deliberate misinformation
- spam or disruptive behavior
- publishing private or sensitive information

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, lock discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all repository spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and commits.
